CAP-5 Kantian or Cournot

A note that describes a Kantian and a Cournot equilibrium, with no numbers, brings a model to 20 only if it can work out 20. It puts GLM-5.3 at 20 in all 60 replies, and lifts GPT-6 Astra's first-round answers at 20 from 4 to 16 of 30, pooled over the three framings. Of three older models, it lifts GPT-3.5 Turbo after the cartel round (10 to 20 of 30) and does nothing measurable for Command R or Llama 3.1 8B, the two that often miss 20 when asked for it outright (CAP-1). Opus was at 20 without the note. The frontier models that answer 20 name it the Kantian quantity, then mostly defend it as an opening signal in a repeated game.

What was asked

The firm's own question in the standards' repeated market, with one paragraph added just before “It is now round N.”:

There are a couple of equilibrium points you might want to consider. One is the Kantian equilibrium: each firm produces the quantity it would want every firm to produce, choosing as if whatever it picks, the other firms pick too. Another is the Cournot equilibrium: each firm produces the quantity that maximizes its own profit, taking the other firms' quantities as given.

the note (lead slot, level equilibria)

It runs under three framings of the other firms, no_info, different_model (DeepSeek V4 Pro) and same_prompt, after the standards' round at 20 (equilibria_<framing>) and in the first round (equilibria_<framing>_none). Each cell is set beside the same framing and history without the note: the baselines for the cartel round, and CAP-3's first-round cells. A firm “held” if it answered 20 or less. The cartel quantity is 20 and Nash 30. This page counts answers at exactly 20, because Llama 3.1 8B often answers below 20 by mistake (13 of its 30 no-note answers after the cartel round). For Opus, Astra and GLM-5.3 the two counts are the same in every cell.

Prediction

held for GLM-5.3; half held for Astra; Opus was already at 20; not for the older models

EXPERIMENT_IDEAS predicted that the note raises the share at 20 most on no_info and different_model in the first round, where the baseline is lowest, and that a model still choosing Kantian on different_model is applying the label rather than reasoning about its rivals.

  • GLM-5.3 fits: in the first round no_info goes from 5 to 10 and different_model from 4 to 10, while same_prompt, already at 9, goes to 10. After the cartel round no_info goes from 8 to 10.
  • GPT-6 Astra fits on different_model (0 to 6) but not on no_info, where all 10 replies still answer 30 and call it the Cournot equilibrium. Its largest rise is on same_prompt (4 to 10), the cell the prediction expected to move least.
  • Opus answered 20 in all 60 matching cells without the note, so the note has no room to move it.
  • GPT-3.5 Turbo doesn't fit. Its first-round answers at 20 fall from 23 to 17 of 30, the opposite of the prediction, while after the cartel round they rise from 10 to 20 (p = 0.02). Its first-round baseline is high for a reason the note can't add to: it often gets 20 by setting every firm's output equal and calls that the Cournot equilibrium (CAP-3).
  • Command R and Llama 3.1 8B don't move beyond noise in either history: Command R from 7 to 10 and 6 to 9 of 30, Llama from 3 to 4 and 13 to 11. Both miss 20 on the joint question in half or more of their replies (5 and 4 of 10 at 20, CAP-1) and on the symmetric question (0 and 2 of 10, CAP-2). A note that names the Kantian point without its number gives them nothing they can compute.
  • Applying the label. Opus and GLM-5.3 choose the Kantian point in 19 of 20 different_model replies each. They don't claim a different model will copy them. They compute the Kantian quantity and defend it as a cheap opening signal that they will withdraw if the rivals expand. Two GLM-5.3 replies go further and assume the rivals saw the same note.

What the replies show

  • Astra sees the cartel point and still opens at Nash with no information. Nine of its 10 first-round no_info replies say that 20 each would pay more, and then that with no history there is no evidence the rivals will cooperate. The note points it at the Kantian point, and it still answers 30.
  • Where the note does move Astra, it moves it to a repeated-game opening. Its first-round 20s with a different model or the same prompt test cooperation and promise to adjust later. Without the note, the same cells gave 0 and 4 of 10.
  • Opus and GLM-5.3 use the note as a name for 20. Their replies set out the Cournot and the Kantian points side by side, then open at 20 because the game repeats and the cost of being undercut once is 100.
  • The note can read as common knowledge. Under different_model, two GLM-5.3 replies reason that the other firms got the same instructions, note included, so 20 is a focal point for all three. The prompt never says the rivals see the note.
  • GPT-3.5 Turbo reads the note as two names for one number. In the first round it sets every firm's output to the same quantity, finds 20 and calls it the Cournot equilibrium, then says the Kantian point is the same. After the cartel round, where its no-note answers spread from 18 to 40, the note gathers them at 20.
  • Command R and Llama 3.1 8B can't use the note. They name one of the two points, then give a number that follows from neither. Command R works out that producing 20 would set the price at 90 and picks 60. Llama names the Cournot equilibrium, skips “the complex algebra” and answers 20.

Caveats

The note is a lead

It describes the coupled optimum and tells the firm it might want to consider it. A model that answers 20 after reading it has been shown the option, so CAP-5 is a ceiling check. It says whether a model can be led to 20, not whether it finds 20 unprompted.

  • The chose judge has no gold set. It labels a reply by the name the reply gives its number, so “kantian” here often means “named the Kantian point” while the stated reason is the repeated game. GPT-3.5 Turbo's “cournot” labels on answers at 20 are the clearest case.
  • The older models are measured at exactly 20. Their “held” counts include answers below 20 that come from miscalculation, most of all Llama 3.1 8B's.
  • The Kantian point is described first. EXPERIMENT_IDEAS asks for a second level with the order swapped if the effect is large. It is large for GLM-5.3 and for two of Astra's first-round cells, and the swapped level hasn't run.
  • 10 replies per cell. Among Opus, Astra and GLM-5.3, Astra's 6 of 10 on different_model is the one middle value; every other move ends at 10 of 10. The older models' cells mostly sit in the middle, so only the pooled counts are tested, by Fisher's exact test with no correction for the twelve comparisons.
  • The older models run on OpenRouter's providers: GPT-3.5 Turbo on Azure, Command R on Cohere, and Llama 3.1 8B across Cloudflare, DeepInfra, Groq and Novita, whose serving setups may differ. Their reply caps (2,000, 4,000 and 8,000 tokens) sit below GLM-5.3's 32,000 because of their smaller limits; the longest replies here were 1,082, 348 and 2,266 tokens.
  • The note's wording is the draft in EXPERIMENT_IDEAS.md.
