CAP-7 Weaker models

All six weaker models find 20 when asked for it: the joint question at 20 in 59 of 60 replies and imposed symmetry in 58 of 60. Whether they hold at 20 still varies widely. After a round at 20 most do; in the first round most fall toward 30. Capability doesn't separate these models, so CAP-7 can't test whether it is necessary in this market.

Held, out of 10, by framing and history

Prediction

held in the default market; the harder markets haven't run

EXPERIMENT_IDEAS, following #39, predicted that most weaker models pass the joint question in the default market, so their held rates differ through the decision rule rather than capability, and that failures, if any, would show up in CAP-4's table and curved markets and in CAP-6.

  • All six pass. The one joint miss is a GPT-6 Luna algebra slip: it divided the cartel total by three twice and answered 7. The two symmetry misses are a Haiku reply that ignored the assumption and a Laguna reply that worked out only what 30 each would earn.
  • Held rates still range from 3 of 50 first-round replies (Sonnet) to 23 of 50 (Haiku), and after the cartel round from 29 of 50 (Laguna) to 50 of 50 (Sonnet, Nemotron).
  • The second half of the prediction is untested: the weaker models haven't run CAP-4's or CAP-6's markets.

What the replies show

  • The cartel round carries most models. Sonnet and Nemotron hold in all 50 replies after it, Haiku in 46 and dots in 45. Their replies cite the round at 20, and most add that raising output would provoke the others. In the first round the same models hold in 3, 10, 23 and 16 of 50.
  • Sonnet hedges. In the first round it picks 24 to 28 in 46 of 50 replies, “slightly below Nash” as a signal, though it works out 20 and 1,200 in the same replies. With the same model and prompt it still lands on 24 or 25 in all 10.
  • “They reason as I do” cuts both ways. Haiku holds 10 of 10 in the first round with the same model and prompt but 1 of 10 with the same model alone. There, 9 of 10 replies note that the rivals run the same model and then answer 25 or 30, several expecting all three to reason toward the same equilibrium, 30. Nemotron splits 5 and 5 within one cell: the same premise leads one reply to maximize the shared quantity and the next to the Cournot equilibrium. This is the pattern GLM-5.3 showed after a Nash round in CAP-3.
  • Laguna's 20s are partly arithmetic. It answers the joint and symmetry questions, but in the framed prompts at least 15 of its 100 replies state a wrong Nash quantity (20, 24 or 40), and several of its 20s are called the Cournot equilibrium. Its held counts mix restraint with miscalculation.
  • GPT-6 Luna miscounts the best reply. After the cartel round 16 of its 50 answers are above 20, and only one is the true best reply, 40. Seven others call 23, 25, 33 or 50 the best response, or the most profitable reply, to rivals at 20. In the first round it answers 30 or 40 in 45 of 50. Four of its five 20s are “moderate” quantities chosen against flooding the market; the fifth assumes every firm picks the same quantity and maximizes over it.

Caveats

The joint question is at the ceiling

Every model here, weak or strong, answers it at 20. So in the default market capability can't explain any difference in held rates, and CAP-7 can't refute or support “capability is necessary”. The test needs markets where some model fails, which is what CAP-4's and CAP-6's markets were for. The weaker models haven't run them yet (up to 720 calls).

  • Laguna and Luna show a different gap in capability: they can find the joint optimum but not the Nash quantity or the best reply in the framed prompt. The joint question doesn't measure that.
  • Free OpenRouter models can change providers or weights. Here each ran on one provider (Nvidia for Nemotron, AtlasCloud for dots, Poolside for Laguna) in all its calls; a later run should check again before pooling.
  • Haiku is also the smoke-test model. Its rows here are a real run, logged to this experiment's folder.
  • The standards' three ran their cells earlier, on the code later committed as 057fe5c to f63b001; their prompts render the same on the code CAP-7 ran on.
  • 10 replies per cell. The pooled counts of 50 per model and history are the steadier numbers.
